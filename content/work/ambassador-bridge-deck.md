## The bridge

The Ambassador Bridge is an international long-span suspension bridge connecting the United States and Canada, with a main span of 1,850 ft and a total length of about 7,500 ft. Its grid deck was replaced while the bridge stayed open to traffic.

## What I did

I designed bridge components and reviewed thousands of shop drawings, about 75 drawing sets, for the deck replacement.

Shop-drawing review is the last check before steel is cut. I identified fabrication and installation errors at that stage, which saved hundreds of thousands of dollars in construction cost and significant time.

## What it shows

Careful review is not glamorous, and it is where the money is saved. The same attention to verification is what I now build into software.
