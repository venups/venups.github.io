## The bridge

The Ambassador Bridge is an international long-span suspension bridge connecting Detroit, in the United States, and Windsor, in Canada. Its main span is 1,850 ft and its total length is about 7,500 ft. Its grid deck was replaced while the bridge stayed open to traffic.

## Why the review matters

Replacing a deck under traffic leaves little room for a part that does not fit. On a job like this the pieces are fabricated off site and installed in limited work windows. A part that arrives with the wrong hole pattern or the wrong dimension costs that window, and the correction is made in the field at field prices.

Shop drawings are the fabricator's own drawings of exactly what will be made. Reviewing them is the last check before steel is cut.

## What I did

I designed bridge components and reviewed thousands of shop drawings, about 75 drawing sets, for the deck replacement.

I identified fabrication and installation errors at that stage, while they were still on paper. That saved hundreds of thousands of dollars in construction cost and significant time.

## What it shows

Careful review is not glamorous, and it is where the money is saved. The same attention to verification is what I now build into software such as [Steel Bridge Pal](/work/steel-bridge-pal), where the validation step exists for the same reason.
