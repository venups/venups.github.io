## The project

The interchange improvements add the missing connections between I-278 and US Route 1&9 to improve traffic flow and safety. Bridge No. 2 is a three-span steel bridge.

## My role

I led the structural team through preliminary design, with responsibility for schedule, budget, progress reporting and coordination with NJDOT.

- Delivered the structural alternatives matrix, the alternatives analysis and the cost estimate to WSP, and answered its technical questions.
- Performed the preliminary girder design and structural-depth evaluation.
- Provided substructure forces to the geotechnical team.
- Addressed Seismic Design Category B criteria and connection design, including drilled shafts.
- Advanced the Structure Recommendation Report with the external design team.

## What it shows

This is the management side of the work: owning a scope, a budget and a client relationship, and turning engineering options into a recommendation a client can act on.
