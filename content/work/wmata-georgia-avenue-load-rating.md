## The bridge

The Georgia Avenue Outbound Bridge is a simple-span floorbeam through-girder railway bridge. It is heavily skewed and carries two unique tracks, which puts it outside the assumptions that simple line-girder rating methods rely on.

## What I did

I built a refined STAAD.Pro finite element model of the structure and load rated it to AREMA standards, using the inspection reports for the as-found condition and following WMATA's submission requirements.

## What it shows

When a structure does not fit the textbook method, the answer is a better model, checked carefully. Railway rating to AREMA was new to me on this project, and I learned the standard and delivered to it.
